# Front desk & staff guidelines

Short rules every staff member should follow. Print this and keep it at the front desk.

## Accounts and passwords

- Use **your own** account. Never share logins. The activity log records your name on everything you do.
- Change the temporary password the admin gives you right away. Don't write it on a sticky note.
- Lock the screen or sign out when you leave the desk. The app signs you out after 15 minutes idle anyway.

## Registering a member

1. Ask for a valid ID to confirm name and birth date.
2. Read each health screening question aloud. If any answer is **yes**, advise the member to get medical clearance from a doctor, and tick the confirmation box.
3. Let the member (or parent/guardian for minors) read the Terms, Waiver and Privacy Notice, on screen or printed, before ticking the consent boxes. **Never tick consent for them.**
4. Marketing consent is optional. Don't pressure members.
5. Give the **temporary PIN** to the member privately, in person or by SMS to their own number. It is shown only once.

## Privacy at the counter

- Don't read a member's details out loud, and don't turn the screen toward other people.
- Don't give member information to anyone over the phone or chat, including "family members", without verified consent.
- Health information is confidential. Admins see the details, and staff see only whether clearance is needed.
- Don't photograph screens or export data to personal phones or chat apps.
- Members who ask about their data, or want it deleted, can use the member portal (Profile → Privacy & data), or you can refer them to the DPO.

## Payments

- Give the payment acknowledgement **and** the BIR invoice for every payment.
- Never ask members to send card details by chat or SMS.

## Safety

- Know where the first-aid kit and AED (if any) are, and the emergency numbers (911).
- Report broken equipment right away, and tag it "out of order".
- Report injuries and incidents to the manager the same day, and write them down.

## If something goes wrong

Lost laptop, wrong person got a file, someone used your account? Tell the DPO or admin **immediately**. See [INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md). Speed matters because there is a 72-hour reporting window.
